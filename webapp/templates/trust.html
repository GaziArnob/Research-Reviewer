Trust & privacy

How {{ brand }} treats your papers, and what it cannot do. We would rather state a limit than hide it.

What happens to a paper

  • The PDF is processed and then deleted. Only the extracted results are kept, against your account, until you delete them.
  • Other users cannot open your papers. Every paper page checks ownership on the server.
  • You can delete one paper, or your whole account, at any time under Settings. Account data can be exported as JSON.
  • The audit trail records who did what and when. It never stores manuscript text.

Language model and third parties

  • The optional language-model pass runs on a model you host (Ollama). Nothing is sent to an AI provider.
  • The online checks contact Crossref, Semantic Scholar, arXiv and OpenAlex with the paper title, one abstract sentence and reference titles or DOIs. Turn them off for venues that discourage third-party services. The venue form tells you when that applies.

Venue rules on AI

Each venue template records what the venue's own reviewer page says about AI tools and when we last checked. ICML, ACL Rolling Review, CVPR and IEEE forbid AI-written review text, so their forms hide language-model content. ICLR allows LLMs as a general assist tool with the reviewer fully responsible. NeurIPS and AAAI pages checked were silent. Rules change: check the venue's current page.

What the tool cannot do

  • It does not rate papers. In our benchmark on 20 ICLR 2017 papers the local model's scores had no useful relationship with human ratings (rank correlation −0.19). Scores, ratings and recommendations are therefore never prefilled.
  • Many findings are keyword hints and say so. A missing keyword is not proof that something is missing.
  • A small local model writes generic comments and can miss real weaknesses. The quote check removes unsupported comments but cannot add good ones.
  • Reference matching uses Crossref, which lacks many preprints and books, so some references are reported as "not matched" although they exist.
  • It is not a plagiarism or AI-text detector.

This page describes how the software behaves. It is not legal advice.
