Data we hold

Account data (name, email, password hash), the results extracted from papers you submit, your questions about papers and their answers, and an audit trail of actions. We do not store the uploaded PDF after processing.

Why

To provide the review assistance you ask for and to keep the service secure.

Sharing

Papers are not shared with other users. If online checks are on, the paper title, one abstract sentence and reference titles or DOIs are sent to Crossref, Semantic Scholar, arXiv and OpenAlex. No data is sent to an AI provider when the language model is self-hosted.

Your rights

You can export your data and delete papers or your account under Settings. Contact {{ support }} for anything else.
